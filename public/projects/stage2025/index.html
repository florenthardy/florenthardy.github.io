Summary :
To validate my Masters in Computer Networks, I studied the power consumption of Intel’s P4 programmable switch : the Tofino 1 & 2.
Detail :

ICube’s (University of Strasbourg, CNRS) Network Reserach Group welcomed me to take part in the GARDEN project which aims to study the power consumption of programmable networks.

My internship helped prepare my Ph.D which also takes part in the GARDEN project. My first mission was to study the impact the P4 code has on the consumption of the switch.
